Employee Privacy Notice

Payroll Desk · Last updated November 17, 2026

This notice explains what personal information the company keeps in Payroll Desk, why, who can see it, and what your rights are under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Who is responsible

Eastland Management Consultancy and Assistance Services Co. ("the company") decides what is kept and why, so it is responsible for your information.

Questions or requests go to the data protection officer: [name of data protection officer], [email or phone].

What we keep

InformationWhy
Full name, position, work location, biometric ID, active or inactive statusTo identify you and match your time records
Daily or monthly pay rate and pay group (weekly or semi-monthly)To compute your pay
Daily attendance (present, absent, half day), hours worked, overtime, late minutes, leaveTo compute your pay
Deductions, cash advances and instalments, funds contributions, payment recordsTo compute your net pay and keep a record of what was paid
Home address, contact number or email (only if provided)To print on your payslip and reach you about pay

We keep only what payroll needs. We do not store government ID numbers, bank account numbers or health information in Payroll Desk.

Where it comes from

From you and from company records, and from the biometric time-clock report that is imported into Payroll Desk.

Who can see it

  • Authorized payroll staff, each with their own sign-in and an authenticator code.
  • One access level can view payroll but not change it. Another, for attendance encoding, can see employee names and enter attendance and hours only. It cannot see pay rates, deductions or payroll amounts.
  • Government agencies and others, only when the law requires it.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

The data is kept in a cloud database (Supabase). The site is delivered through Cloudflare. These providers process the data for the company and only on its instructions. Servers may be outside the Philippines. Connections are encrypted, and access is limited to signed-in staff.

Payslips and reports that staff export are files on their own device. They are covered by the company's rules on keeping that device and those files safe.

Cookies and tracking

Payroll Desk has no advertising or analytics trackers. Your browser stores only what is needed to keep you signed in and to remember the light or dark display choice.

How long we keep it

For as long as needed for payroll, tax and labor-law record keeping: [retention period set by the company with its accountant]. After that it is deleted or made anonymous.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, object to how it is used, and ask for it to be blocked or deleted where the law allows. We cannot delete payroll records we are legally required to keep. You may also ask how the data is protected, and you may complain to the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) if you believe your rights were not respected.

If something goes wrong

If a security incident affects your information, the company will assess it and notify the National Privacy Commission and affected employees as the law requires.

Changes

If this notice changes, the new date will appear at the top.
